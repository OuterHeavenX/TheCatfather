# Pawfellas: The Furry Underworld

## Setting

New York City, 1928. The streets are ruled by two ruthless feline syndicates:
**The Claw-stra Nostra** (led by Don Corle-meow-ne) and **The Alley Syndicate**
(run by the mysterious Carmela). A truce has held the city's back alleys
together for years, but everything falls apart when a shipment of grade-A
imported catnip vanishes from the docks.

## The Premise

You play as **Jimmy "Two-Times,"** a fast-talking white cat with a penchant for
double-speaking his way out of trouble. When the missing catnip threatens to
spark an all-out turf war across the city, Don Corle-meow-ne calls you in to
investigate. To solve the heist, you must navigate a web of colorful
characters, execute high-stakes heists, and decide where your true loyalties
lie.

## Key Factions & Characters

### The Bosses

**Don Corle-meow-ne** rules from the shadows, giving demands you can't refuse,
while **Carmela** coordinates her side of the city with quiet, terrifying
precision.

### The Muscle

Heavy hitters like **Al Catpone**, **Sonny "The Hammer,"** and **Sammy "The
Bull"** handle the street-level muscle work, while **Vinnie "The Hook"** handles
anyone who steps out of line.

### The Specialists

**Mobby "The Weasel" DeNiro** handles covert pantry break-ins, **Frankie "The
Lefty"** uses lightning-fast speed for quick escapes, and **Lucky Claw-ciano**
brings miraculous good fortune to every dangerous job.

### The Informants

**Johnny "Tight-Lips"** barely speaks a word, but **Nicky "The Squeaker"** will
spill everything if given enough wet food. **Bugsy Meow-sie** causes erratic
3:00 AM distractions to clear out security, while **Lefty "Ruggiero"** is
constantly getting stuck behind locked doors during key missions.

## Gameplay Loop

Build your crew, balance syndicate diplomacy, and run tactical stealth-action
missions across rooftop alleys, fish markets, and parlor rooms to uncover who
really stole the nip before the city burns down.

Which faction will you side with, or will you take over the underworld
yourself?

# Pawfellas: Core Game Loop

## 1. Strategic Setup & Empire Management

Each game day starts at your desk in the speakeasy. You allocate territory,
set protection fees, and manage syndicate overhead before sending your crew
out into the city.

- **Tariffs:** Set tax rates on imported goods moving through your turf (e.g.
  Catnip, Fancy Salmon, Leather Furniture). High tariffs boost daily income
  but raise tension with rival syndicates and increase the chance of merchant
  rebellions.
- **Payouts:** Pay your crew to maintain loyalty and keep morale high. Muscle
  like Al Catpone and Sonny "The Hammer" demand heavy cuts to stay happy.
  Failing to pay your lieutenants leads to betrayal or territory loss.

## 2. Tactical Operations & Street Execution

Send specific Mobster Cats on daily missions around the neighborhood based on
their unique traits.

- **Collections:** Send reliable crew members like Connie "The Don" or Carmela
  on daily routes to gather routine protection money from local
  establishments.
- **Shakedowns:** Deploy heavy enforcers like Vinnie "The Hook" or Sammy "The
  Bull" to lean on uncooperative business owners, forcing them to pay
  back-taxes or turn over high-value inventory.

### Business Venues for Extortion & Rackets

| Business Type | Protection Yield | Shakedown Risk | Unique Loot / Perks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Blind Pig Bar | High Cash Flow | Moderate | Speakeasy intel; bribing local alley cats |
| Piazza Fruit Market | Moderate Cash Flow | Low | Exotic shipping crates; smuggler hideouts |
| The Docks Fishmonger | High Food Supplies | High | Grade-A Salmon; high-tier health items |
| Luxury Tailor Shop | Rare Items | Low | Silk suits & hats; stat boosts for crew |
| The Corner Hardware Store | Low Cash Flow | Low | Crude weapons (hammers, crowbars, pipes) |

## 3. End-of-Day Ledger & Rival Response

Balance the books at night. Calculate net profits after paying crew cuts and
local police bribes. High extortion rates cause rival factions (like Carmela's
Alley Syndicate) to hit back, forcing you to defend your establishments or
risk losing your daily revenue stream.

## 4. The Long Game

The day cycle above is one afternoon. Underneath it sit four systems that only
move over weeks, and they are what turns a run into a campaign.

### Energy and Nerve

Every cat has **energy**, which is their whole day in points and comes back at
dawn. A venue job takes four of it, a session at the gym takes three, and a job
in The Racket takes between two and eight. So a cat can work a block *or* put
muscle on *or* pull a job — rarely all three — and every morning is an
allocation problem rather than a to-do list.

**Nerve** is yours, not theirs: one pool for the whole house, spent only on The
Racket, refilled by a fixed amount overnight and by nothing else. Money cannot
buy it. It is the ceiling on how much crime a day can contain.

### The Alley Gym

Stats are no longer handed out by levelling. They are put on at the gym, one
small session at a time, paid for in a cat's energy and your money:

| Regimen | Trains |
| --- | --- |
| The Heavy Bag | MUSCLE |
| The Fire Escapes | SNEAK |
| Etiquette Lessons | CHARM |

Every point already trained makes the next one both smaller and dearer, so the
wall a cat runs into is time, not treats. A tin of catnip doubles one session.

### The Racket

Twelve solo jobs in four tiers — PETTY, ORGANISED, SERIOUS, THE BIG SCORE —
that resolve the instant you commit them rather than waiting for nightfall.
Each costs nerve and one cat's energy, tests one stat, and pays a range.

Repeating a job is what makes it safe: every run banks experience in that
crime, which both improves its odds and counts toward the tier's total.
**Banked experience is the only thing that opens the tier above it.** Money
never does.

Botched jobs raise heat, and some of them end with the cat picked up. A jailed
cat is out of everything — jobs, the gym, The Racket — until the days run down
or you post bail at seventy treats a day.

### The Fence and the Holdings

Prices move every night, so the Fence is somewhere you watch rather than a
shop: gear and consumables can be bought when they are cheap and sold back
when they are dear, at the fence's cut both ways.

Holdings are bought once and paid out every night, and each one quietly bends
a rule:

| Holding | Rule it bends |
| --- | --- |
| The Flophouse | +1 ENERGY a day for every cat |
| Cassoni's Garage | +12% on every job in The Racket |
| The Bathhouse | Wounds heal a day sooner |
| The Social Club | +3 NERVE a night, and a higher ceiling |
| The Back-Room Bank | HEAT falls 4 faster a night |
