# Factions, contacts and story relationships

Status: faction relationships are proposed. MAIN has one global `tension` number, `align` (`don`, `carmela`, `self`) and story flags. It has no trust/fear/debt/contact records. Retaliation always uses the Alley Syndicate regardless of alignment. Story flags are stored but not consulted by later gates.

The Claw-stra Nostra under Don Corle-meow-ne and Carmela's Alley Syndicate should remain distinct organizations with contextual motives. Preserve the existing endings, speaker IDs and nine-chapter mystery as campaign material. Reframe it as an initial arc rather than letting day 16 automatically exhaust “become the Catfather.”

## First relationship slice

Introduce a contact through a location and connect one favor to a real reward. Nicky can be a hired informant and a story/contact identity, but recruitment must not reset his relationship. The Don and Carmela remain bosses rather than new recruits. Preserve any legitimately grandfathered hires from very old saves until a specific migration policy is agreed.

Trust measures willingness to cooperate; fear measures coercive leverage; favor balance tracks a specific obligation. Add respect or suspicion only when an action consumes or changes them meaningfully. A friendly bartender may introduce a dockworker; frightening the same bartender may yield cash but dry up voluntary tips. Show the causal reason for the change.

## Factions later

Track each faction independently. An action can earn the Don's trust, Carmela's respect and both houses' suspicion for different reasons. Do not force their values to sum to 100. Preserve alignment as a historical choice; do not retroactively equate it to all future relationships. Street respect, business relationships and police heat answer different questions and should not collapse into one morality score.

A job should identify who commissioned it, who benefits, who may discover it and which obligations follow. Start with explicit authored consequences; no simulation of every NPC knowing every fact. Add a bounded evidence/discovery mechanic only when double-dealing missions need it.

## Story integration

Replace forced morning queues gradually with pending invitations, conversations, evidence and news at known locations. Existing flags such as `paid_nicky`, `shook_nicky`, `met_carmela` and `refused_carmela` can become meaningful prerequisites or dialogue variants. Use separate availability, accepted, resolved and claimed states; replaying dialogue must never repay a reward. Do not require an unavailable specialist without an alternate route.

Convert one beat at a time and migrate `story_seen` into resolved state. Keep old endings accessible and do not replay already-resolved costs. Verify all three existing branches and the path that manipulates both factions before writing a longer campaign.
